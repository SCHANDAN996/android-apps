# Graphics Specs — मिस्त्री कैलकुलेटर

Two required Play graphics: **App icon (512×512)** and **Feature graphic (1024×500)**. Specs below are exact and ready to hand to a designer or an AI image tool.

---

## 1. APP ICON — 512 × 512 PNG (32-bit, no alpha transparency for the master)

**Concept:** a mason's **trowel crossed with a calculator**, inside a rounded orange square. Instantly says "construction + calculation", and is visually distinct from the sibling apps (Kisan = green land/₹ theme, SpeakEasy = blue speech theme).

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Background | Solid deep orange **#E65100**, or a top-left→bottom-right gradient **#FF6D00 → #E65100** |
| Shape | Rounded square (Play auto-masks; keep key art within the safe centre ~66%) |
| Main symbol | A white **mason's trowel** (diagonal) + a small **calculator grid** (3–4 keys) overlapping at the base |
| Symbol colour | White **#FFFFFF**, with a subtle warm shadow for depth |
| Accent | One brick/amber tile in **#FFCA28** (amber) as a small highlight — ties to the in-app accent |
| Style | Flat, bold, minimal; thick strokes; NO text in the icon; NO thin lines (must read at 48px) |
| Padding | ~12% inner padding so nothing touches the edge |

**One-line prompt for an AI icon generator:**
```
Flat modern app icon, rounded square, deep orange (#E65100) background, a white mason's trowel crossed with a simple white calculator, one amber (#FFCA28) brick accent, bold minimal, no text, centered, high contrast.
```

**Distinctiveness check:** orange + trowel/calculator ≠ any other app in your portfolio. ✅

---

## 2. FEATURE GRAPHIC — 1024 × 500 PNG/JPG

Shown at the top of the listing and in promos. No essential content in the outer 10% (Play may overlay a play button / crop on some surfaces).

**Layout (left → right):**
- **Left 55%:** headline text on the orange background.
- **Right 45%:** the app icon or a phone mockup showing the Home grid + one result card.

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Background | Deep orange **#E65100** → **#FF6D00** diagonal gradient |
| Headline (line 1) | `मिस्त्री कैलकुलेटर` — white, bold, ~64px |
| Headline (line 2) | `घर बनाने का पूरा हिसाब — 100% ऑफलाइन` — white/amber, ~34px |
| Sub-pills (optional) | Small rounded chips: `ईंट` · `सीमेंट` · `सरिया` · `प्लास्टर` · `टाइल्स` · `पेंट` |
| Right art | App icon (large) OR a phone frame showing the Home screen |
| Font | Bold Devanagari (Noto Sans Devanagari / Poppins Devanagari) |

**Ready-to-use headline text (copy-paste):**
```
मिस्त्री कैलकुलेटर
घर बनाने का पूरा हिसाब — 100% ऑफलाइन
ईंट · सीमेंट · सरिया · प्लास्टर · टाइल्स · पेंट
```

**English alt (if you also make an English feature graphic):**
```
Mistri Calculator
Full construction estimate — 100% offline
Bricks · Cement · Steel · Plaster · Tiles · Paint
```

**Policy note:** no "#1 / best" claims, no competitor logos, no fake buttons or "Install" arrows drawn on the graphic (Play disallows misleading UI in the feature graphic).
