# Screenshots Plan — मिस्त्री कैलकुलेटर

- **Count:** 8 phone screenshots
- **Size:** 1080 × 1920 (or 1080 × 2400) PNG/JPG, portrait
- **Style:** Real app screenshot inside a light frame, deep-orange (#E65100) caption bar at the TOP with white bold Hindi text. Keep captions 3–6 words, large font (readable as a thumbnail).
- **Rule:** The **first 2** screenshots carry the full value proposition (most people decide from these two).

> Tip: Fill the app with realistic sample values before capturing (e.g., 10ft × 10ft wall, M20 slab, rates set) so results look useful, not empty.

## Layout notes
- Caption bar height ≈ 260 px at top; app screenshot below it.
- Font: bold Devanagari (e.g., Noto Sans Devanagari / Poppins Devanagari), white on orange.
- Keep a thin consistent margin; same template for all 8 so the set looks like one family.
- Do NOT show test-ad banners in screenshots — capture with ads hidden or crop the banner out (screenshots must not look like ads / must reflect real UI).
- Optional badge on #1 and #8: a small "📴 100% ऑफलाइन" pill.
