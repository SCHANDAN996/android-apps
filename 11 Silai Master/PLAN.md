# 11 — Silai Master (Priority: LOW)

## Ek line mein
Tailor ka digital measurement book — customer ka naap save karo, order track karo, Hindi mein, offline.

## Target User
- Gents/ladies tailor (gaon se sheher tak)
- Boutique chalane wali mahilayen
- Silai seekhne wale students

## Problem
Tailor customer ka naap diary mein likhta hai — diary kho gayi to customer ka naap gaya. Purane customer ka naap dhundna mushkil. Order kab dena hai yaad nahi rehta. English tailor apps hain lekin Hindi + simple + offline nahi.

## MVP Features (v1)
1. **Customer list** — naam, phone, photo (optional)
2. **Naap (measurement) save** — ready templates:
   - Gents: kurta-pajama, shirt, pant, sherwani
   - Ladies: blouse, salwar-kameez, lehenga, frock
   - Custom fields bhi add kar sake
3. **Order tracking** — kapda liya, kab dena hai, kitna advance, kitna baki
4. **Delivery reminder** — kal 3 order dene hain notification
5. **Rate card** — apni silai rates ki list
6. **Naap WhatsApp share** — dusre karigar ko naap bhejna ho to

## Baad ke Features (v2+)
- Naap ke diagrams (kahan se kahan napna hai — silai students ke liye)
- Photo attach (design ka reference photo order ke saath)
- Hisab summary (mahine mein kitna kamaya)

## Screens
1. Home — aaj/kal ke pending orders + customer search
2. Customer detail — uske saare naap + order history
3. Naap entry screen (template select → fields bharo)
4. Order entry screen
5. Rate card

## Engine Reuse
- 03/04 wala database layer — reuse
- UI framework — reuse
- Measurement templates hi asli naya kaam hai

## Monetization
- Banner + interstitial
- ₹149 lifetime (50 customers ke baad unlock) — tailor ka business tool hai, dega

## ASO / Keywords
- silai app, tailor measurement app hindi, naap book, darzi app, boutique order book
- Naam suggestion: "Silai Master - Naap & Order Book"

## Competition
- Kuch tailor apps hain (Tailor Book etc.) — is list mein sabse zyada competition isi ka hai, isliye priority sabse last. Lekin Hindi-first + offline + ladies-wear templates strong differentiator hai

## Kitna Time Lagega
2-3 hafte

## Success Metric (3 mahine)
- 5,000+ installs
- Paid conversion — tailor audience business tool ke liye paise deti hai
