# 00.1 — Aage Badhne Se Pahle: Final Suggestions

Ye 8 baatein plans mein nahi likhi thi. Inhe padhkar hi shuru karo.

---

## 1. SABSE PEHLE: SpeakEasy aur Kisan Calculator ko LAUNCH karo (naya kuch mat shuru karo)

Ye sabse badi salah hai. Aapke paas 2 apps **already lagbhag bane hue** hain, aur dono MASS APP wale hi niches mein hain (English + Kisan). Naye 11 apps shuru karne se pehle:

- In dono ko Play Store par publish karo
- Poori pipeline ek baar seekho: developer account → 20-tester closed testing → production → ASO → AdMob setup → pehla payout
- In dono ka 2-3 mahine ka data dekho — ye **free mein batayega ki poori MASS APP strategy chalegi ya nahi**

Adha bana app ₹0 kamata hai. 11 naye adhoore apps se 2 launched apps behtar hain. **MASS APP folder tab tak sirf planning hai jab tak ye 2 live nahi hote.**

## 2. Pipeline ka one-time setup pehle karo (har app mein kaam aayega)

Ek baar banao, 13 baar use karo:

- **Privacy policy website** — GitHub Pages par free, har app ki policy ka URL yahan se (Play Store mandatory mangta hai)
- **Chhota tester group (optional lekin zaroori)** — hamara account organization hai isliye 20-tester rule lagu nahi hota, lekin 5-10 asli users (dost, family, target audience) ka WhatsApp group banao jo har app launch se pehle use karke bugs bataye
- **AdMob account** — abhi banao, approve hone mein time lagta hai
- **Ek developer brand naam** — sab apps ek pehchan se aayen (jaise "XYZ Apps"), icons ka style family ek jaisa — Play Store par trust banta hai
- **Signing keystore ka backup** — pehli app banate waqt jo keystore file banegi, uska backup 2-3 jagah rakho (Google Drive + pen drive). Keystore kho gaya = app ka update kabhi nahi de paoge

## 3. Shared "core" Flutter package banao — yahi asli mass-production hai

Pehle 2-3 apps ke baad ek internal package nikalo jisme ho:

- Ads wrapper (banner/interstitial/rewarded + frequency control ek jagah)
- Rating dialog (sahi moment par)
- "Hamare aur apps" cross-promo widget
- Hindi number/date formatting
- Local DB layer + backup/restore
- Settings/language screen

Iske baad naya app = sirf content + 2-3 naye screens. Ye package hi aapki factory hai.

## 4. Har app se pehle 2 din ka validation karo (3 hafte bachane ke liye)

Code likhne se pehle:

- Play Store par uske Hindi keywords search karo — top 5 results ke installs aur reviews padho
- Reviews mein complaints dhundo ("kaash offline hota", "Hindi nahi hai") — yahi aapka feature list hai
- Us audience ke YouTube channels/WhatsApp groups mein jhanko — wo asli mein kya mangte hain

Agar validation mein lage ki demand nahi hai — us app ko list se kaat do. Plan pathhar ki lakeer nahi hai.

## 5. Har app mein Day-1 se analytics dalo (bina data ke winner nahi dikhega)

Firebase Analytics (free) har app mein pehle din se:

- Installs, DAU, Day-7/Day-30 retention
- Kaunsa feature use ho raha hai (kaunsa calculator, kaunsa lesson)

3 mahine baad "kaunsa app winner hai" ka faisla data karega, feeling nahi. Bina analytics ke aap andhere mein teer chalaoge.

## 6. AdMob account ko jaan se sambhalo

- **Apne ads par KABHI click mat karo** — na khud, na dost-family se karwao. AdMob invalid-traffic ban = poore portfolio ki earning ek din mein zero, aur wapas nahi milta
- Development ke time hamesha **test ads** use karo
- Ads ki bharmaar mat karo — har screen par interstitial = policy strike + users bhaag jayenge

## 7. Pace realistic rakho — 11 apps ka matlab 11 mahine, 3 nahi

- 1 app = 2-3 hafte code + 1 hafta launch/testing + reels banana. Realistic: **1 app har 5-6 hafte**
- Saal bhar mein 6-8 apps achhe se > 11 apps jaldbaazi mein
- Burnout is raste ka sabse bada silent killer hai. Hafte mein 1 din code se poora door raho
- Har launch ke baad us app ke folder mein ek `SEEKHA.md` likho — kya chala, kya nahi. 5th app tak aap expert ban chuke hoge

## 8. Asli bada paisa B2B mein chhupa hai — aankhen khuli rakho

Jab koi app chal jaye to sirf ads par mat ruko:

- **Dudh ka Hisab** chala → dairy collection centers ko white-label bech sakte ho
- **Bachat Gat** chala → NGO/NRLM blocks bulk mein le sakte hain
- **Hajiri** chala → chhote builders ko direct
- **Interview English** chala → coaching institutes partnership

Ads se ₹20k/mahina, lekin 2-3 B2B clients se ₹50k+ — Bharat apps ka asli scale aksar yahi hota hai.
