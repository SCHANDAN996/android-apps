# 00 — KADVI SACHAI: Kya Isse Sach Mein Earning Hogi?

Seedha jawab: **Haan ho sakti hai, lekin jitna log sochte hain usse bahut kam, bahut der se, aur bahut zyada mehnat ke baad.** Neeche poori sachai numbers ke saath hai. Ise motivational post mat samjho — ye planning document hai.

---

## SACHAI #1: Pehle 6 mahine earning lagbhag ZERO hogi

- Naya app Play Store par publish karte hi koi download nahi karta. ASO ranking mein 3-6 mahine lagte hain.
- AdMob ka payment threshold $100 (~₹8,500) hai — pehla payment aane mein hi 4-8 mahine lag sakte hain.
- Zyada-tar indie apps 1,000 installs bhi cross nahi karte. Ye normal hai, failure nahi.

**Matlab:** Pehla saal "investment period" hai. Is dauran ghar chalane ka doosra source zaroor rakho.

## SACHAI #2: India ka ad rate duniya mein sabse kam hai

Real math dekho (ek app ke liye):

| Cheez | Number |
|---|---|
| Total installs | 10,000 |
| Daily active users (DAU) | ~800 (8% hota hai utility apps mein) |
| Ads per user per day | 2-3 |
| Daily impressions | ~2,000 |
| India interstitial eCPM | $1-2 (₹85-170 per 1000) |
| **Daily earning** | **₹170-340** |
| **Monthly earning** | **₹5,000-10,000** |

Ye hai 10,000 installs wale ek *achhe-chalte* app ki kamai. Banner ads se to iska 1/4 bhi nahi milega.

**Matlab:** ₹50,000/mahina chahiye to portfolio mein total 60,000-1,00,000 active-user-baar chahiye — yani ya to 1 app 1 lakh+ installs wala, ya 8-10 apps 10-15k installs wale.

## SACHAI #3: 11 mein se 8-9 apps flop honge — ye plan ka hissa hai

Research data (ek solo dev ne 28 apps banaye): **1 app ne ~99% revenue diya, baki 27 ne milkar 1%.**

- Ye strategy ki haar nahi hai — ye lottery tickets hain. Har app ek ticket hai.
- Galti ye hogi ki kisi flop app par 3 mahine aur laga do "thoda aur polish kar doon" sochkar.
- **Rule:** App launch ke 3 mahine baad agar 3,000 installs nahi → usko chhodo (maintain karo, naya kaam mat karo). Jo app uth raha hai, saara time usi par lagao.

## SACHAI #4: Google Play ke rode (ye sabse zaroori section hai)

1. **20-tester rule — HUM PAR LAGU NAHI HOTA ✅:** Personal developer account par har naye app ko production se pehle 20 testers ke saath 14 din closed testing karni padti hai. **Lekin hamara account ORGANIZATION account hai — is par ye rule nahi lagta.** Ye mass production ke liye bahut bada advantage hai: app ready → seedha production release possible.
   - *Phir bhi salah:* Har app ko production se pehle 5-10 asli users se test zaroor karwao (chahe internal testing track par) — bugs ke saath launch = kharab reviews = ASO barbad.

2. **Repetitive content policy:** Google ek jaise dikhne wale template apps ko SPAM mankar reject/strike karta hai. 3 English apps agar bilkul same UI + same structure hue to account par strike aa sakta hai — **account ban hua to saare apps ek saath gaye.**
   - *Solution:* Har app ka UI theme, icon style, content depth alag rakho. Engine reuse karo, *dikhawat* reuse mat karo. Ek hi din mein 3 apps publish mat karo — har app mein 3-4 hafte ka gap rakho.

## SACHAI #5: "Publish karo aur bhool jao" se kabhi earning nahi hogi

Apps khud nahi badhte. Jo karna hi padega:

1. **ASO har app ke liye** — Hindi keywords title/description mein, Hindi screenshots, chhota demo video. Ye 1 din ka kaam hai per app, skip mat karo.
2. **YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels** — ye sabse bada free lever hai. Aapki audience (mistri, kisan, dairy wale, job seekers) din bhar reels dekhti hai. Har app ke liye 15-30 second ke 8-10 shorts banao ("Eent ka hisab 10 second mein"). Hafte mein 2-3 shorts — consistency 6 mahine.
3. **WhatsApp share loop** — har app mein result share karne ka button (plans mein already hai). Bharat mein apps WhatsApp se failte hain, Play Store search se nahi.
4. **Reviews mangna** — app mein sahi moment par (3rd successful use ke baad) rating dialog. 4.3+ rating ke bina ASO nahi chalta.
5. **Cross-promotion** — har app mein "Hamare aur apps" section. Free traffic hai, chhodna paap hai.

## KITNA MIL SAKTA HAI — 3 Scenarios (12-18 mahine baad)

| Scenario | Probability | Monthly Earning | Kab |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kharab** (sirf publish kiya, marketing nahi ki) | 50-60% | ₹0 - 5,000 | Kabhi nahi badhega |
| **Realistic** (11 apps + ASO + reels + consistency 12 mahine) | 30-40% | ₹15,000 - 40,000 | Month 12-18 se |
| **Achha** (1 app hit ho gaya, 1 lakh+ installs) | 10-15% | ₹50,000 - 1,50,000+ | Month 12-24 |

Aur paid unlock (₹99-199 IAP) wale apps (04 Hajiri, 09 SHG, 10 Bhada, 11 Silai) ads se zyada de sakte hain agar users business wale hain — 1% conversion bhi ads se behtar hai.

## TIMELINE — Kya expect karo kab

```
Month 0-3:   3 apps live (01, 02, 03). Earning: ₹0. Feeling: "kuch nahi ho raha" — NORMAL hai.
Month 3-6:   6 apps live. Pehla AdMob payout (~₹8,500) shayad aaye. Reels se pehla spike dikhega.
Month 6-9:   Data bolega — kaunsa app uth raha hai. Losers ko freeze karo, winner par lago.
Month 9-12:  Winner app par focus + baki portfolio passive. Earning: ₹10-30k/mahina (agar sab sahi chala).
Month 12-18: Winner ko scale karo (naye features, aur bhashayen). Yahan se hi asli kamai shuru hoti hai.
```

## AAPKE FAVOUR MEIN KYA HAI (taaki sirf daraana na ho)

1. **2 engines ready hain** — SpeakEasy + Kisan Calculator. Aapka per-app cost sabse kam hai, yahi aapka asli edge hai.
2. **Voice-practice apps copy karna mushkil hai** — calculator koi bhi bana lega, offline voice pipeline nahi. 02/07/08 defensible hain.
3. **Bharat niches mein bade players nahi aate** — mistri/dudh/pashu jaise niches unke liye chhote hain, aapke liye kaafi bade.
4. **Aap Hindi audience ko samajhte ho** — ye Silicon Valley wale nahi samajhte.

## FINAL NICHOD

- **Earning hogi?** Haan, lekin 12-18 mahine consistency ke baad, aur guarantee kisi ki nahi.
- **Kitni?** Realistic target: Month 12-18 tak ₹15-40k/mahina. Hit lag gaya to ₹50k-1.5L+.
- **Sabse bada risk?** Google account strike (Sachai #4) aur beech mein chhod dena (sabse common failure).
- **Sabse bada lever?** Reels/Shorts marketing — code se zyada earning isi se decide hogi.
- **Rule of thumb:** Ye lottery nahi, kheti hai — buwai (12 mahine) ke bina fasal nahi.
