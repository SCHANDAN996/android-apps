## PART 1 — NICHE SELECTION: Kaun Sa Aur Kyun

### 1.1 Pehle wo niches jo maine REJECT kiye (aur kyun)

Ye zaroori hai, kyunki YouTube par yahi 3 niches sabse zyada suggest hote hain — aur teeno aapke liye trap hain.

| Niche | Data kya kehta hai | Reject kyun kiya |
|---|---|---|
| **Health / Weight Loss** | Sabse zyada CTR — **3.02%** (sabse upar) | Meta ki sabse sakht policy. 2026 mein before/after ban ab "implied transformation" tak badh gaya. "Kya aap pareshan hain" jaisi second-person language BAN hai. Supplements category mein **Q1 2026 mein 64% accounts review hue** (+41% QoQ). Ek AI-likhi health book = liability + account ban |
| **Make Money Online / Trading** | Sabse zyada demand, sabse zyada price tolerance | 2026 se **38 deshon mein advertiser verification mandatory**, income claims regulated. India mein SEBI angle alag. Ek strike = pura ad account gaya. Aapke paas backup account nahi hai |
| **AI Productivity / Global E-learning** | E-learning CTR **2.74%**, YoY growth **+29.38%** — sabse tez badhta category | Yahan aapka **koi edge nahi**. English content, US CPM 10x, aur AI-slop saturation sabse zyada yahin hai — **2026 mein 30%+ naye titles par AI disclosure hai**, buyers ka trust toot chuka hai. Aap wahan 500th competitor honge |

> **Nichod:** Jo niche sabse "obvious" hai, wahi sabse mehnga aur sabse risky hai.

### 1.2 Jo niche maine CHUNA: Thekedar / Contractor Kit

**Ye aapke `01 Mistri Calculator` app ka hi audience hai.**

Chunne ki 6 wajah — ek-ek karke:

**1. Competition lagbhag ZERO hai.**
Meta ke feed mein contractor ke liye koi info-product ad nahi chal raha. Har D2C brand, har edtech Instagram par ladta hai. Aap Facebook Feed par Hindi mein akele honge. **Kam competition = kam CPM = kam lagat.** Ye seedha aapki "kam lagat" wali requirement hai.

**2. Buyer ke paas asli paisa hai.**
Yahan target *mistri* (majdoor) nahi hai — **thekedar** hai. Jiska mahina ₹50k–₹3L ka turnover hai, jo 5-20 aadmi rakhta hai, aur jo ₹299 ka business tool bina soche kharidta hai. Ye difference poori strategy ka center hai.

**3. Aapko ye audience already samajh aati hai.**
Aapne Mistri Calculator, Hajiri Majdoori, Jameen Napi banaye hain. Aap unki bhasha, unka hisab, unka dard jaante ho. **KADVI SACHAI doc mein aapne khud likha tha — "Aap Hindi audience ko samajhte ho, ye Silicon Valley wale nahi samajhte."** Yahi wo edge hai, use karo.

**4. Meta policy ka koi landmine nahi.**
Na health, na income claim, na finance. Sirf business tools. Ad account safe rahega. **Ye sabse under-rated fayda hai** — aapka pura app business ek Google account par tika hai, ek Meta ban afford nahi kar sakte.

**5. AI ye content SAHI bana sakta hai.**
Construction ke material ratios (M20 = 1:1.5:3, plaster = 1:6, etc.) **standard aur verifiable** hain — AI inhe sahi banata hai aur aap IS-code se check kar sakte ho. Ye "AI slop" nahi banega. (Iske ulat, mandi rate ya labour rate AI ko nahi pata — isliye product design mein wo blank rakhenge, neeche Part 4 dekho.)

**6. Ebook buyer = App user (DOUBLE REVENUE).**
Kit ke andar Mistri Calculator app free bonus hoga. Har ₹299 wala buyer ek installed, engaged app user ban jaayega. Aapki KADVI SACHAI wali problem — "installs nahi aa rahe, ASO mein 3-6 mahine lagte hain" — ka **paid shortcut** yahi hai.

### 1.4 "Single ya Multiple?" — Ka Faisla

**Single. Aur ye technical wajah se hai, discipline ki wajah se nahi:**

- Meta ka pixel ek offer par **conversion signal concentrate** hone se hi seekhta hai. 2 offers = aadha data = dono learning phase mein atke rahenge
- ₹500/din ke budget mein 2 funnel chalane ka matlab hai ₹250/din per funnel — us par Meta ka algorithm kabhi optimize nahi kar paayega
- Research: **$1,000/din se kam budget par Advantage+ 31% underperform karta hai** — kyunki learning phase 17-25 din tak khinch jaata hai

**Par isse "factory" ki tarah banao:** Thekedar Kit ka har asset (landing page, funnel, ad script, PDF template, checkout) aisa banao ki naam aur content badal kar Dairy Kit 1 hafte mein ban jaaye. **Pehla funnel product hai, doosra se aage system hai.**

### 2.4 Payment & Delivery Setup (ads se PEHLE zaroori)

| Cheez | Recommendation | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout | **Razorpay** ya **Cashfree** | UPI mandatory hai. India mein 70%+ payment UPI se. Gumroad/Stripe India mein friction hai |

## PART 4 — CONTENT KAISE BANAYEIN (Part A: EBOOK)

### 4.1 Sabse pehle: AI-slop se bachne ka design

**Problem:** 2026 mein 30%+ naye titles AI-disclosed hain. Buyers ko generic AI content turant pehchan aata hai → refund, bura review, Facebook page par gaali. Aur aapka pura content AI se ban raha hai.

**Solution — ye product design se solve hoga, mehnat se nahi:**

> **"Bharo aur chalao" design.** Kit ka har page ek **template/format/formula** hai, koi "gyan" nahi. Rates, mazdoori, mandi bhav — sab **BLANK** hain, jo user apne area ka bharega.

Iske 3 fayde ek saath:
1. AI ko wahi karne diya jo wo sahi karta hai — **structure, formula, format** (jo standard aur verifiable hai)
2. Wo cheez nahi maangi jo AI galat karta hai — **local rates, current numbers**
3. Refund ka sabse bada karan (galat number) khatam — kyunki numbers user ke apne hain
4. Perceived value **zyada** ho jaati hai: "gyan wali book" se "kaam ka tool" behtar bikta hai

**Ye "kam mehnat" ka jugaad nahi hai — ye asli mein behtar product hai.** Thekedar ko lecture nahi chahiye, use ready Excel chahiye jisme wo apna rate daal kar client ko quotation bhej de.

### 4.3 Production pipeline (aap developer ho, iska fayda uthao)

**Canva mein mat banao — 60 page manually design karne mein 3 din lagenge.** Iske badle:

```
Step 1 →  AI se Markdown mein pura content likhwao (1 din)
Step 2 →  Ek HTML + CSS template banao (print stylesheet ke saath)
Step 3 →  Script: Markdown → HTML → PDF
          (Puppeteer / wkhtmltopdf / Flutter ka pdf package)
Step 4 →  Excel sheets alag — Google Sheets template link + .xlsx download
Step 5 →  Ek baar template ban gaya → Dairy Kit, Silai Kit 1 din mein
```

**Yahi wo "factory" hai.** Pehli kit mein 5 din lagenge, doosri mein 1 din.

### 4.4 QC Checklist — ye pass kiye bina publish mat karo

- [ ] Har formula ko ek real example se khud calculate karke check kiya
- [ ] Material ratios kisi civil engineer / IS code se verify kiye
- [ ] Koi bhi guaranteed income/profit claim PDF mein nahi hai
- [ ] Contract format par legal disclaimer hai
- [ ] Excel formulas 3 alag inputs par test kiye
- [ ] PDF mobile screen par readable hai (thekedar laptop par nahi kholega)
- [ ] File size < 15 MB (4G par download hona chahiye)
- [ ] Har page par aapka WhatsApp support number hai
- [ ] Kisi copyrighted image ya doosre ki book ka content nahi hai

## PART 6 — METRICS & KILL RULES

Roz subah ye 5 number dekho, aur kuch nahi:

| Metric | Target | Agar target se neeche |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook rate** (3s view / impressions) | > 25% | Creative ke pehle 3 second badlo |
| **Link CTR** | > 1.5% | Hook theek hai, offer/copy kharab hai |
| **LP → Initiate Checkout** | > 8% | Landing page ka problem — price, speed, ya trust |
| **IC → Purchase** | > 40% | Checkout ka problem — UPI option, form ke fields |
| **CPA** | < ₹150 | Neeche wale kill rules dekho |

### Kill Rules (bina emotion ke lagao)

- Ek creative par **₹300 kharch, 0 Add-to-Cart** → band karo
- Ek creative par **CPA target se 3x, 0 purchase** → band karo
- Ad set par **₹3,500 kharch (7 din), ROAS < 0.5x** → offer ya landing page badlo, creative nahi
- **Kisi bhi ad set ko 3 din se pehle mat chhedo** — learning phase reset ho jaata hai. Ye sabse common galti hai

> Aapke `00 KADVI SACHAI` doc ka wahi rule yahan bhi lagta hai: *"Galti ye hogi ki kisi flop par 3 mahine aur laga do."* Ads mein ye 3 din hai, 3 mahine nahi.

## PART 8 — IMAANDAAR RISK LIST

1. **Pehla funnel fail ho sakta hai — ye normal hai.** ₹3,500 fees hai seekhne ki. Zyadatar log pehla funnel fail hone par chhod dete hain — asli fail wahi hai
2. **Thekedar audience PDF nahi padhti.** Isliye Excel + video walkthrough hai, sirf PDF nahi. Agar refund zyada aayein to product video-first karo
3. **AI content ka risk asli hai.** Galat formula = bura review = page barbaad. Part 4.4 ka QC checklist skip mat karo
4. **Meta ad account restrict ho sakta hai.** Din 1 se: business verification karao, page par asli content rakho, aur **ek backup page pehle se bana kar rakho**
5. **Ye AdMob se tez hai, par "aasan" nahi hai.** AdMob mein 12-18 mahine lagenge (aapka apna anumaan). Ye 30-60 din mein bata dega ki chalega ya nahi. **Tez feedback loop hi iska asli fayda hai — guaranteed paisa nahi**
6. **Sabse bada upside chhipa hua hai:** har buyer aapka app user hai. Agar funnel break-even par bhi chala, to aapne **paid app installs muft mein** khareed liye — jo aapki asli problem hai
