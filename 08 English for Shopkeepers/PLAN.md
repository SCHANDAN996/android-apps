# 08 — English for Shopkeepers (Priority: MEDIUM)

## Ek line mein
Dukandar ke liye customer-handling English — daam batana, mol-bhav, saman samjhana — bolkar practice, offline.

## Target User
- Kirana, kapda, mobile shop, medical store ke dukandar
- Salesman/shop staff
- Chhote hotel/dhaba wale (tourist areas mein khaas demand)

## Problem
Dukandar ko English-speaking customer ke saamne jhijhak hoti hai — bade showroom ka staff English bol leta hai, chhota dukandar customer kho deta hai. General English apps unke kaam ke sentences (daam, discount, quality, guarantee) nahi sikhate.

## MVP Features (v1)
1. **Situation-wise lessons**:
   - Customer ka swagat ("How can I help you?")
   - Daam batana, discount ki baat
   - Saman ki quality/guarantee samjhana
   - Size/color/variety dikhana
   - Paisa lena, change dena, bill banana
   - "Nahi hai" politely bolna + alternative dena
2. **Bolkar practice** — voice pipeline
3. **Number bolna** — rate/price English mein bolna (two fifty, one ninety-nine) — dukandar ki sabse badi need
4. **Cheat sheet** — counter par rakhe phone mein turant dekhne wali list

## Baad ke Features (v2+)
- Dhaba/hotel pack (menu English)
- Medical store pack (dawai counter English)

## Screens
SpeakEasy jaisa hi — Home (situations) → Lesson (sun/bol/score) → Cheat sheet → Progress
