# 07 — English for Drivers & Delivery Boys (Priority: MEDIUM)

## Ek line mein
Ola/Uber driver aur Zomato/Swiggy/Amazon delivery walon ke liye customer se baat karne ki English — bolkar practice, offline.

## Target User
- Cab drivers (Ola, Uber, Rapido)
- Delivery partners (Zomato, Swiggy, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit)
- 20-40 saal, tier 1/2 sheher mein kaam karne wale Hindi-speaking gig workers

## Problem
Gig worker ko roz English-speaking customers milte hain — "Where are you?", "OTP please", "Leave at door" — samajh nahi aata to rating giri, tip gayi. Inke liye koi targeted app nahi hai. General English apps inke kaam ke sentences nahi sikhate.

## MVP Features (v1)
1. **Situation-wise lessons** (SpeakEasy format):
   - Customer ko call karna ("I have reached", "Please share location")
   - Address samajhna, OTP mangna
   - Late hone par kya bolna
   - Complaint/gussa customer handle karna
   - Tip/rating politely mangna
2. **Bolkar practice** — voice pipeline se pronunciation check
3. **Sun kar samjho** — customer ke common sentences sunao, Hindi meaning dikhao (listening skill — inke liye sabse zaroori)
4. **Cheat sheet** — 50 ready sentences ki offline list (ride ke beech turant dekh sake)
5. Driver mode / Delivery mode alag content pack

## Baad ke Features (v2+)
- Security guard / office boy pack (same engine, aur audience)
- English se Hindi quick-translate phrasebook

## Screens
1. Home — apna kaam chuno (Driver/Delivery) + lesson list
2. Lesson screen (SpeakEasy jaisa: sun, bol, score)
3. Cheat sheet screen (search ke saath)
4. Progress screen

## Engine Reuse
SpeakEasy engine — 90% reuse. Sirf content JSON naya. Interview English (02) banane ke baad ye template ban jayega, phir ye app 1 hafte ka kaam hai.

## Monetization
- Interstitial lesson complete par
- Rewarded ad se advanced packs unlock
- Ye audience paid nahi karegi — pure ads app hai, isliye installs volume chahiye

## ASO / Keywords
- english for drivers, delivery boy english, english bolna sikhe, customer se baat english me, spoken english hindi
- Naam suggestion: "Driver-Delivery English - Bolkar Sikhe"

## Competition
- Zero targeted competition. General spoken-English apps bahut hain (isliye ASO mein "driver/delivery" specific keywords par khelo, "spoken english" par nahi)

## Kitna Time Lagega
1-1.5 hafte (02 ke baad)

## Success Metric (3 mahine)
- 10,000+ installs (gig workers ka WhatsApp group sharing strong hota hai)
- Cheat sheet screen ka usage — agar wo hit hai to v2 usi par focus karo
