# 12 — Tourch Lite (Utility App)

## Ek line mein
Lock button ke double-press se ON aur OFF hone wala offline-first torch app.

## Target User
- Kisan jo raat ko khet mein kaam karte hain aur turant light chahiye.
- Drivers aur delivery agents jo andhere mein kaam karte hain.
- Normal users jinhe emergency mein bina phone unlock kiye fast torch chahiye.

## Problem
Emergency mein phone unlock karke notification panel down karke torch dhundhna slow aur complex hota hai. Physical shortcuts (jaise double tap power button) direct use karne ka koi simple, clean aur bina ads se bhara app nahi milta.

## MVP Features (v1)
1. **Interactive Toggle Switch** — App kholte hi bada retro or glowing bulb animation switch.
2. **Double Tap Lock Button** — Phone locked state mein bhi power button double tap/press karne se light turant ON.
3. **Double Tap Lock Button to Turn OFF** — Flashlight ON hone par power button double tap/press karte hi light OFF.
4. **Smart Notification Control** — Notification panel se direct service aur torch toggle ka setting.
5. **No Internet Permissions Required** — User trust ke liye pure offline utility app with zero data tracking.
6. **Detailed Instruction Guide** — How to disable default system camera double-tap and exclude from battery saver.

## Monetization
- Minimal banner ad at the bottom.
- Ad-free version at ₹49/lifetime for clean utility tools.
