### 📋 STEP-BY-STEP GENERATION WORKFLOW

Go through the following steps systematically to generate a world-class Play Store launch kit.

---

#### STEP 1: APP TITLE & SHORT DESCRIPTION (ASO & Conversion Optimization)
Analyze the keywords and generate highly optimized titles and short descriptions.

- **Title (Strictly Max 30 Characters):** Provide 3 options. Integrate the primary keyword within the first 15 characters. Rank them by organic search potential.
- **Short Description (Strictly Max 80 Characters):** Provide 3 high-converting options. Use the **PAS framework** (Highlight the main problem and state the app as the immediate solution). Use a strong action verb (e.g., "बचाएं", "नापें", "हिसाब करें").

---

#### STEP 2: FULL DESCRIPTION (Proven Copywriting Framework - Max 4000 Characters)
Write a masterfully crafted Full Description using the **AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)** model. 
*Ensure a natural Keyword Density of 1.5% to 2% (do NOT stuff). Maintain a highly readable format with bullet points and bold headers.*

**Structure to follow:**
1. **ATTENTION (The Hook):** A bold, localized opening paragraph (2-3 lines). Address the target user's direct daily struggle (e.g., "क्या आपको भी खेत नापने या ब्याज का हिसाब लगाने में परेशानी होती है?").
2. **INTEREST (The Agitation):** Explain why traditional/manual calculation methods lead to costly mistakes.
3. **DESIRE (The Solution):** Introduce the App. Show how it makes their life 10x easier.
4. **CORE FEATURES (Feature Breakdown with Emojis):**
   - Detail 4-5 major features. Use a clean emoji for each. Write a 2-line explanation highlighting the *benefit* of each feature, not just the technical input.
   - Examples of features: Land conversion, crop profit estimation, tractor/hire booking, simple interest manager.
5. **TRUST & OFFLINE ADVANTAGE:** Highlight that the app works **100% offline** (no internet needed in rural areas) and keeps all data completely private and safe on their device.
6. **ACTION (Call to Action - CTA):** A warm, compelling closing encouraging them to install now.

---

- **Recommended Category:** (e.g., Finance or Tools).
- **15 Search Keywords:** Grouped by search volume (High, Medium, Low) for search listing indexing.

---

#### STEP 4: SCREENSHOT DESIGN PLAN & COPYWRITING

- **Visual Scene:** Exactly what UI screen is shown, what values/calculations are pre-filled (use realistic, relatable numbers, e.g., local farm names/amounts), and device placement.
- **Headline overlay Copy (Strictly Max 5 words):** A bold, punchy caption (written in the target language) to print on top of the image (e.g., "खेत नापें बिल्कुल सटीक!").
- **Sub-caption (Strictly Max 8 words):** Secondary text explaining the screen value (e.g., "बीघा, एकड़ और हेक्टर में तुरंत बदलें").
